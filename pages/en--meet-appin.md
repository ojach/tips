## So, who is Appin?

Appin is a character born from OJapp's home screen icon.

My job is to make things OJapp has actually tested—PWAs, home screen behavior, Web apps, and all the little browser quirks—easier to understand.

I especially like home screens.

## Where you'll usually find me

- OJapp Tips articles
- PWA guides and explanations
- Anything about the home screen
- Places where you might wonder, “Why does it work like that?”
- Results from OJapp's experiments

If you spot me, just think, “Oh, there he is again.”
