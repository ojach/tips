
With the rise of ChatGPT and AI writing tools, creating blog posts has become easier than ever.
But the reality is this: **AI-generated articles often fail to rank well in search engines.**

In this article, we break down why AI content underperforms and how to turn AI-assisted writing into
**high-ranking SEO content**.



## 1. The biggest reason AI articles don’t rank: They become “averaged content”

AI models generate the most statistically common answer based on huge datasets.
This means the output naturally becomes **generic and unoriginal**.

### Typical signs of averaged AI content

- Feels similar to other articles on the web
- Lacks E-E-A-T (experience, expertise, authoritativeness, trust)
- No real opinions or insights
- No personal anecdotes or unique data

 	
 	
 	
 	

From 2024–2026, Google increasingly penalizes low-originality pages.
Pure AI content falls into this trap almost immediately.



## 2. Readers want “human experience” — something AI cannot generate

Among the E-E-A-T elements, the strongest ranking signal is
**Experience — actual human involvement.**

AI has no real experiences, so its writing lacks emotional detail, context, and authentic insights.

AI writing often sounds like:

- “It is generally said that…”
- “In many cases…”
- “Typically…”

 	
 	
 	

These phrases feel hollow and weaken user trust — and Google notices that.



## 3. The web is now flooded with AI-generated content

In 2026, millions of AI articles are published daily.
The competition among AI-style content is extremely high, and most of it looks the same.

If your article is “just another AI post,” it will get buried.



## 4. AI cannot fully understand “why the user is searching”

AI is great at generating information, but it struggles with the core of SEO:
**understanding the searcher’s real intention.**

Readers are not just asking “what is〇〇？”。
They ask because they have a problem they want solved.

AI often provides surface-level answers that miss this deeper intent.



## 5. How to make AI-assisted content rank: the winning formula

### ① Let AI create the structure — you add experience

AI: outline, headings, general explanation
You: real examples, screenshots, firsthand experience

Combining AI’s speed with your uniqueness fixes the “generic content” problem instantly.



### ② Always include a “this is my experience” section

- Your experiments
- Your results
- Your failures and corrections
- Your screenshots

 	
 	
 	
 	

Google heavily rewards human-originated insights.



### ③ Add a human voice to the intro and conclusion

Examples:

- “When I actually tried this, I found…”
- “This part confused me at first.”
- “If you’re struggling with〇〇, I get it — I was there too.”

 	
 	
 	

These instantly break the AI-like tone and pull the reader in.



### ④ Identify competitors’ weak points — fill the gaps with your expertise

Most AI-generated articles cover the same general points.
To stand out, review the top-ranking pages and look for:

- Missing explanations
- Outdated data
- Incorrect assumptions

 	
 	
 	

Then write the part they failed to write.
This single step dramatically improves ranking potential.



### ⑤ Use a human-written conclusion

AI summaries are too neutral:

“In conclusion, it can be said that…”

A human summary is opinionated and relatable:

“After actually trying this during my own site growth, here’s what mattered most.”

Google recognizes and rewards this authenticity.



## Conclusion: AI isn’t weak — but AI-only content is

- AI articles tend to become generic and unoriginal
- Readers (and Google) want real human experience
- The web is oversaturated with AI-only content
- AI doesn’t fully understand deep search intent
- AI + your experience = high-ranking SEO content

 	
 	
 	
 	
 	

AI isn’t a replacement for human writing —
it’s a **multiplier for your originality**.
Once you add your unique experiences, AI-assisted content becomes incredibly powerful in SEO.
