## Why OJapp Splitter Uses Only “Square” and “3:4”

Unlike many image-editing apps that offer every possible aspect ratio, OJapp Splitter intentionally limits the options to two:

- **Square:** Ideal for icons, SNS thumbnails, profile photos
- **3:4:** Matches almost all smartphone photos and feels natural

 	
 	

These two ratios cover more than 80% of real-world mobile use cases, allowing the tool to stay fast, simple, and high quality.



## 4 Practical Mobile Use Cases for OJapp Splitter

### 1. Create perfectly aligned Instagram posts

Instagram looks simple, but its automatic cropping is strict and often unpredictable.
Using the **3:4** ratio keeps compositions stable and prevents accidental cropping.

With OJapp Splitter, you can easily create:

- 3-split vertical posts
- 6-split tall layouts

 	
 	

Your images will stay aligned when previewed on your feed, reducing the “why does it look slightly off?” problem.



### 2. Check and adjust wallpaper composition

Smartphone wallpapers never display exactly as you see them—iPhones especially crop the top and bottom slightly.
Splitting an image into Square or 3:4 helps you visualize which areas will be safely visible.

This is particularly useful for lock screens that require extremely precise placement.



### 3. Make Before/After comparison images instantly

Beauty, DIY, cooking, and review posts often need a simple “side-by-side” comparison.
With Splitter:

**Square + 2-split** = perfect Before/After collage in one tap.

No editing apps, no complex layouts—just clean, consistent output.



### 4. Organize photos by unifying aspect ratios

Your camera roll may contain photos in many different aspect ratios, making feeds or albums look inconsistent.
If you want a uniform aesthetic—especially for Instagram grids—use:

- **Square** for a unified grid look
- **3:4** for natural photo layouts

 	
 	

Splitter helps you standardize everything quickly.



## Tips for Using Splitter Smoothly on Smartphones

### 1. “Long-press → Save to Photos” is the fastest method

When saving split images on iPhone or Android, long-pressing the output image and choosing
**“Save to Photos”**
is the quickest and most reliable way.

### 2. Avoid using the browser’s “Download” feature

Especially on iPhones, downloaded files often go into the Downloads folder—not the Photos app—causing confusion.
Long-press save ensures the images go directly to the camera roll.

### 3. 3:4 is the safest ratio for SNS use

If you're unsure which ratio to choose for social posts, 3:4 is the most platform-friendly and least likely to be cropped unexpectedly.
