# Shortcut Method vs OJapp: A Complete Comparison for Custom iPhone Icons

When it comes to customizing iPhone Home Screen icons, the most well-known solution is using the **Shortcuts app**.

However, many users quickly notice that **the Shortcut method can be confusing, slow, and difficult to recommend to others.**

In this guide, we compare the Shortcut method and the OJapp approach across five key factors— **ease of setup, shareability, speed, consistency, and safety**— to help you understand which method is better for your needs.

## What Is the Shortcut Method?

The Shortcut method uses the built-in iOS “Shortcuts” app to create a custom icon that launches a URL or app via an automation-like shortcut.

While highly customizable, it comes with several drawbacks—especially for beginners.

## Advantages of the Shortcut Method

- Allows complete freedom when choosing icons
- Uses built-in iOS functionality—no installation required
- Supports URL schemes for opening apps directly

 	
 	
 	

It works especially well for people who want to **retheme their entire Home Screen with matching icons**.

## Disadvantages of the Shortcut Method

- Setup is complicated and hard for beginners
- Shortcuts cannot be shared as ready-to-use icons（users must recreate the setup）
- A **Shortcuts screen flashes briefly** when launching the icon
- Managing multiple URLs becomes tedious

 	
 	
 	
 	

It’s fine for personal use, but **not suitable for asking customers to “add your shop to their Home Screen.”**

## What Is OJapp?

OJapp is a web tool that lets you freely set **the icon, title, and URL** when adding a website to your Home Screen.

It works on both iPhone and Android. No Shortcuts, no app installation—just a simple, direct setup.

## Shortcut vs OJapp: Comparison Across Five Factors

### 1. Ease of Setup

- **Shortcut:** Long, complex steps. Difficult for beginners.
- **OJapp:** Select an image, enter a URL—anyone can use it instantly.

 	
 	

### 2. Shareability

- **Shortcut:** Users must recreate the setup themselves.
- **OJapp:** Share one URL and the exact same icon settings can be reproduced.

 	
 	

### 3. Launch Speed

- **Shortcut:** A Shortcut confirmation screen appears momentarily.
- **OJapp:** Opens the webpage directly with no delay.

 	
 	

### 4. Consistency & Management

- **Shortcut:** Must be set up on every device; needs recreation after switching phones.
- **OJapp:** As long as you have the URL, the same setup can be restored anytime.

 	
 	

### 5. Safety & Reliability

- **Shortcut:** Incorrect URL or settings can break the shortcut.
- **OJapp:** Simply opens the URL directly—stable and safe.

 	
 	

## Conclusion: Use Cases Make the Difference

**● Want a themed Home Screen or full icon overhaul → Choose the Shortcut method**
**● Want users to add your website or shop to their Home Screen → Choose OJapp**

By choosing the right method, you can optimize your Home Screen without unnecessary complexity.

## Both Methods Are Useful to Know

For anyone interested in Home Screen customization, understanding both the Shortcut method and OJapp gives you the most flexibility.

OJapp solves one specific problem: making it **simple, fast, and shareable** to place a website on the Home Screen using your own custom icon.
